Aerochronix

250 Incident Aviation Intelligence Platform

I built a searchable library of 250 aviation investigations in React and TypeScript and used Three.js for 40 animated 2.5D flight-path reconstructions. The semantic knowledge graph uses a D3 layout to connect incidents through shared factors documented in investigation reports. I optimized rendering by showing a representative set of nodes and limiting background edges to the current view, and tested the graph with 5,000 synthetic incidents and 50,000 connections.

Tech Stack: React, TypeScript, D3.js, Three.js

Branchseed Navigator

Hackathon Winner

Our four-person team won first place in the Battle of the Schools healthcare track with a CPU-only CTA prototype built in 24 hours. I outlined ways to detect abdominal aortic branches, set evaluation criteria, and used what I learned from medical school lectures on YouTube to manually verify two organizer-provided cases. The pipeline searches a narrow shell around the aorta and traces the first 5–10 mm of each detected branch in physical scan coordinates.

Tech Stack: Python, NumPy, SciPy, SimpleITK, Node.js

Candybal

Won Best Use of Meshy

Our four-person team built a two-player cupcake brawl in a three-hour jam. A WebSocket relay through a Cloudflare Tunnel turns iPhone tilt into Unreal Engine controls. I designed the sunlight mechanic, which melts the cupcakes’ frosting and changes when players attack or retreat.

Casentra

Corporate Collapse Simulator

I built Casentra to let players make decisions during corporate collapses and compare the outcomes with the documented record. The live collection has 18 simulations, including Enron, FTX, Silicon Valley Bank, and WorldCom. A deterministic engine replays choices against scenario rules, and Enron Interview Mode scores calculations and gives structured feedback.

Tech Stack: React, TypeScript, Vite, Netlify Functions

Computer Vision Coin Counter

Canadian Coin Recognition on Raspberry Pi

I spent about a month building and tuning a Raspberry Pi 3 counter that recognizes five Canadian coin denominations and totals their value. I applied Gaussian blur for noisy images, tuned Hough circle detection and colour thresholds, and averaged detections across 19 frames to make the readings more consistent.

Tech Stack: Python, OpenCV, NumPy, Raspberry Pi 3

Piano Tiles-Inspired Hardware Game

Four-Button Timing Game

My partner and I built this timing game inspired by Piano Tiles. I wired four physical push buttons through a breadboard to the Raspberry Pi and connected a piezo buzzer. Debounced GPIO inputs drive the Tkinter game loop, and different tones signal hits and misses.

Tech Stack: Python, Tkinter, Raspberry Pi

About me

Hey, I'm Raymond! I’m drawn to aviation and autonomous vehicles because the details matter. Building with cameras, flight data, and physical controls has taught me to test the assumptions that look fine on paper and fail with real inputs.

On the University of Waterloo’s Automotive Future Team (UWAFT), I’m integrating a rear electric motor into a hybrid vehicle model in MATLAB/Simulink, configuring torque commands, axle connections, and feedback. I compare baseline and modified simulations to validate the powertrain, tracking motor torque, vehicle speed, and battery state of charge.
