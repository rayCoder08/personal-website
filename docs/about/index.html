About me.

I’m Raymond Mi, a Computer Science student at the University of Waterloo. I’m interested in how software helps people understand complex systems and make better-informed decisions.

What I’m exploring

Aviation and autonomous vehicles are two places where I keep coming back to the same question: what happened, and what can we learn from the evidence? Aerochronix gave me a place to explore that question through flight data, investigation reports, and interactive visualizations.

I also like the hands-on work of making software respond to the world. Building with cameras, Raspberry Pi boards, and motion sensors means testing with real inputs and working through the cases that a clean demo can miss.

Earlier work

I built a website for my high school’s game development club, with meeting information, resources, and a carousel of nine student-built games.
